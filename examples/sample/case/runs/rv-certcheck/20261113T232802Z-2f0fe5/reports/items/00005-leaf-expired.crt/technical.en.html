Limitations

  • Every chain is judged at one instant (--at, recorded in each report); the same certificate can be valid at one date and expired or revoked at another.
  • Offline, revocation is checked only against the CRLs and OCSP responses supplied (--crl, --ocsp, or found among the evidence); a revocation published elsewhere is not seen.
  • Trust lists are used as dated snapshots (date and SHA-256 recorded); services added, suspended or withdrawn after the snapshot are not reflected.
  • In version 0.1 the trust list's own XAdES signature is not verified; the snapshot's source and SHA-256 are recorded so it can be checked independently. The Colombian list is a curated file, because ONAC publishes no machine-readable list.
  • Certificates issued by Rakvor's internal PKI are for internal use only: they are not a firma digital under Ley 527 de 1999, art. 28 (Colombia), and not qualified certificates under eIDAS (Regulation (EU) No 910/2014).
  • The tool's results have been compared with OpenSSL and Python reference implementations on test sets, but no error rate has yet been measured on a validated corpus.
  • A result without findings does not prove that a file is authentic or unaltered before acquisition.
  • Modification times come from the file system of the examined copy and may differ from the original source.
  • A trusted timestamp of the audit log shows that the log existed in this exact form at the stated time; it does not show that the examined copy matches the original source.
